01 / The project

Frameworking software for small and big ambitions

Framework brings everything needed to design a bicycle frame into one application: geometry, tubes, parts, analysis and fabrication. For home framebuilders and professional workshops alike.

Why

Start from the bike

Designing a frame usually means juggling spreadsheets, general-purpose CAD, tube catalogues and workshop notes. Framework starts from the bicycle, not from generic mechanical software.

How

Everything stays connected

A parametric geometry driven by eleven dimensions, a constraint solver, real tube and part references, then analysis and workshop drawings. Change one dimension and everything updates.

What

A desktop application

It runs offline, in metric or imperial units, with its interface in five languages. Your projects stay on your computer.
