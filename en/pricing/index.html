Questions

Is the beta really free?

Yes. Every feature is free during the beta and the public build.

Are these prices final?

No, these are the planned prices. They will be confirmed before the end of the public build, with the testers' input.

What's the difference between Hobbyist and Professional?

The same design, analysis and render tools. The professional license adds commercial use and what you need to sell: drawings in the workshop's name, size ranges, customer approval, quotes and G-code.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Framework is a desktop application that works offline.
